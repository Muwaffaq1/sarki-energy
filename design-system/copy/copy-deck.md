# Sarki Energy copy deck (v1)

Copy for the 20 page templates. The source in code is `project/components/src/templates.js`; this deck is what Figma should match.

## Voice

The live site's best lines set the voice: "Measure first. Then spend.", "Fuel that arrives. Kit that lasts.", "Straight answers, no sales pitch.", "with arithmetic, not adjectives". Write like that everywhere:

- **Concrete over abstract.** Name the thing (diesel, battery, evening peak, cold chain), not "solutions" or "platforms".
- **Two-beat headlines.** A short claim, then its consequence or contrast. No puns.
- **Plain promises we can keep.** Never state a time, city, warranty or figure the business hasn't confirmed. Unconfirmed facts are written `[confirm: …]` so they can't ship by accident.
- **Sentence case** for every heading, button and label.
- **One verb per action across the site:** "Start a project" (general enquiry), "Book a site survey" (service pages), "Send order request" (cart), "Chat on WhatsApp" (general chat). Specific WhatsApp actions name what you send: "Send your bill on WhatsApp", "Ask an engineer on WhatsApp".

"Advancing your progress." stays the company sign-off: the cover tagline, footer and social bios. It no longer carries the homepage hero, where it says nothing about energy and repeats itself (advancing and progress mean the same thing).

## Homepage hero (recommended)

| | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Eyebrow | Solar · Storage · Fuel · Engineering |
| Headline | **Energy you can plan around.** |
| Lead | Solar and battery systems, fuel supply, and the engineering that connects them, for homes, businesses and industry across Nigeria. We measure your site first, build to standard, and stay accountable after handover. |
| Primary | Start a project |
| Secondary | Explore solutions |
| Note | Site visits are free for homes. |

**Why it fits:** grid outages and fuel prices make energy the least predictable cost in a Nigerian home or business, and every Sarki line of business answers that. The headline echoes the site's own lines ("A bill you can plan", "a schedule you can plan around"), so the promise runs through the whole site. The eyebrow names all four businesses, so oil and gas customers don't read Sarki as a solar-only company.

**Alternatives if you want a different register:**
- *Power that shows up.* Shorter and more emotional, closer to the fuel page.
- *Measured. Built. Kept running.* Process-led, closer to the audit page; weaker on the fuel side.

## Kept as written (already on voice)

- Fuel: "Fuel that arrives. Kit that lasts."
- Audit: "Measure first. Then spend."
- Insights: "Straight answers, no sales pitch."
- Projects: "Work we can stand behind."
- Start a project: "Tell us what you are building." / "Tell us where to come."
- CTA band: "Have a site in mind? We'll come and measure it."
- Error example: "Enter an 11-digit Nigerian number, like 0803 123 4567."
- 404: "This page isn't connected." Light, fits an empty state, and the body text says what to do next.

## Needs the business to confirm before launch

3. Typical installation time for homes, and lead time for pay-once systems.
4. Warranty terms per product.
5. All case-study figures (the Kano rooftop project is a layout example; its numbers are invented).
