The Sarki Energy mark: a lowercase "se" bleeding out of a disc. Vectorised from the original artwork (Figma → Brand → Logo).

- `sarki-mark.svg`: black disc, white letters. Use it on light surfaces.
- `sarki-mark-inverse.svg`: white disc, black letters. Use it on `surface-inverse`, in dark mode, and over photos.
- `favicon.svg`: follows the system theme. `favicon-32.png` and `apple-touch-icon-180.png` are the fallbacks; `sarki-mark-512.png` is for social and app stores.

In product code, use the `Logo` component (it uses the `logo-mark` and `logo-on-mark` tokens). Minimum size 24px. Keep clear space of a quarter of the mark's width on every side. Never recolour, outline, crop or stretch it.
